Melbourne's luxury boutique hotel

Inspired by the golden age of the 1920s, The Royce is a luxury boutique hotel that once housed Melbourne's original automobile showroom, taking its name from one of the grandest cars of that era, the Rolls Royce. From the heritage art deco details in the Showroom Bar, to the handsome discreet opulence that inform the rich interiors, allow us to usher you into a place of unprecedented luxury and tasteful service.

A quiet opulence

Our rooms speak to the Rolls Royce, where discreet luxury informs every surface, and handsome monochromatic tones preside. The tone softens in our suites, sophisticated spaces with an understated sumptuousness, referencing the lofts of New York and the grand hotels of England and Paris.

Drink and Dine

Glamorous we may be, but our dining reflects the spirit of Melbourne - relaxed, welcoming and unpretentious. From breakfast in the conservatory to apero on the boulevard, lunch and dinner to nightcaps by the fireplaces, we welcome you to step inside.
